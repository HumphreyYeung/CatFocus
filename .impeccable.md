## Design Context

### Users
CatFocus is for students and knowledge workers who want help starting and sustaining focused work. They use short focus sessions, breaks, ambient sound, and a virtual cat companion to make consistency feel lighter and more rewarding.

### Brand Personality
Playful, cheeky, and supportive. Luna is lovable and a little lazy: her inner monologue can resist exercise without becoming negative or scolding. The product should feel encouraging rather than clinical or productivity-obsessed.

### Aesthetic Direction
A bright, predominantly monochrome native iOS experience with bold rounded typography, crisp outlines, white media backgrounds, and small purposeful accents. Layouts should be calm and direct, with expressive mascot assets carrying the personality. Avoid decorative gradients, heavy card stacks, excessive shadows, and motion that delays content visibility.

### Design Principles
- Keep one clear primary action per screen and make selected states unambiguous.
- Reuse shared catalogs, tokens, and components so the same content stays consistent across Preset, My Cat, Training, and Paywall.
- Let Luna provide the delight; keep surrounding UI restrained and readable.
- Prefer immediate, stable content over elaborate loading or transition machinery.
- Preserve fixed mascot canvases and support Reduce Motion without changing layout.
